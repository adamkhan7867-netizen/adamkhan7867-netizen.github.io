How I work

The same five steps on every project

Discover

Stakeholder analysis, interviews and the documents people already use.

Model

As-is and to-be BPMN swimlanes and UML use cases that show where work stalls.

Decide

A business case that compares real options and makes one recommendation.

Define

User stories with acceptance criteria, MoSCoW priorities and traceability.

Prove and hand over

UAT scenarios traced to stories, an end-of-project report and an open-actions log.

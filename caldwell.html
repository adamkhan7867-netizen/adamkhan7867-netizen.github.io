CRM and onboarding transformation

An independent training provider held every enquiry and lead in spreadsheets. The goal was a CRM linked to a new landing page, with an 18% first-quarter revenue uplift as the target.

01 InitiationProblem

Spreadsheets were holding sales back

The client earns from two streams, classroom courses and professional exam testing. Neither had a single view of the customer.

  • Inaccurate and duplicated data. Manual entry led to out-of-date and duplicate records.
  • No automation. Every follow-up was typed and sent by hand, so leads went cold.
  • No central view. Interactions sat in separate files with no dashboard for managers.
  • Could not scale. The contact list was set to grow by around 28% within the year.
  • Security and compliance risk. Customer data was saved in many places with no control.

01 InitiationBusiness case

Three CRMs compared, one recommended

I compared each option on CRM capability, ease of use for a team new to CRM, and annual cost for 10 users within the £97k budget.

Salesforce

Highly customisable with strong AI reporting, but costly and complex for first-time CRM users.

£2,400 to £15,840 a year, plus implementation

Mailchimp

Strong email marketing, but no sales pipeline, relationship history or lead scoring.

£2,658 to £5,864 a year, rising after 12 months

Recommended

ActiveCampaign

CRM, email marketing and automation in one place. Easy to learn, supports lead scoring, and integrates with the landing page.

£5,604 to £11,172 a year, covers 10 users and 35,000 contacts

04 DeliveryUser stories

User stories with acceptance criteria

Nine stories across three epics, written from the point of view of each user group. All nine were prioritised as Must Have for the first release.

IDUser storyAcceptance criteriaPriority
Accounts and contact management
US-01As a salesperson, I want to organise contacts effectively, so that I can maximise the value of each customer.
  • Contacts sort A to Z by first name
  • Child accounts link to and show on the parent account, to reveal cross-sell opportunities
  • Customer email is visible
Must
US-02As a salesperson, I want to call customers from the CRM, so that I can close more sales.
  • A phone icon next to each customer starts a call
  • Calls can last up to 2 hours
Must
US-03As a salesperson, I want to follow up by email, so that I can qualify where each customer is in the process.
  • An email is sent automatically when a lead is created
  • The CRM reminds me to contact customers
  • Follow-up tasks are assigned to me with due dates
Must
Lead management
US-04As a sales manager, I want to see my team's progress, so that they stay focused on converting leads.
  • Shows each person's KPIs and conversion rate
  • Red, amber and green scoring, which managers can override
  • A dashboard shows how quickly ready leads are actioned
Must
US-05As a salesperson, I want a dedicated leads dashboard, so that I can keep every lead moving.
  • Lead status changes from a dropdown
  • I can change the red, amber or green score after a follow-up
  • An email goes out automatically if the first contact attempt fails
Must
US-06As a marketer, I want to personalise the customer experience using data, so that customers get more value.
  • Works on web and mobile using current UI patterns
  • Emails and content are tailored to the course the customer joined
Must
Dashboard and reporting
US-07As a marketer, I want to see and act on email campaign performance, so that I can improve revenue.
  • A dashboard section shows campaign results
  • Click into any metric to see which links were clicked
  • Alerts the team and management when a performance threshold is crossed
Must
US-08As a student, I want a dashboard of my course progress and deadlines, so that I can stay on track.
  • Courses are listed by urgency, with module status shown
  • Progress can be exported as a PDF
  • Shows an "Explore new courses" button when I have no active course
Must
US-09As a finance administrator, I want monthly revenue reports by course and centre, so that I can reconcile finances.
  • Filter by date range, course and centre

What went well

  • Wireframes and process diagrams helped stakeholders and developers understand each other, even when meetings were limited
  • Rolling the CRM out in phases reduced risk

What was hard

  • Time zone differences slowed decisions
  • Revenue had not yet moved at close, so the 18% uplift remains a target to track

What I'd do next time

  • Join backlog refinement earlier to spot gaps
  • Align requirements, acceptance criteria and test scripts from day one
  • Keep BA support available after UAT for defect triage

Advertising and sponsorship workflow

A community radio station sells advertising to local businesses for its Ramadan broadcasts. Every advert passes through sales, scripting, production, client approval and scheduling, and the hand-offs were hard to see.

Project summary

My role: discovery through to test design and handover

I gathered requirements from management, sponsors and the production team, mapped the current and proposed process, and wrote a business case for standardising the station's existing Trello board. I then defined the user stories and test scenarios, and built a working Trello prototype of the new workflow.

01 InitiationBusiness case

Fix the tool they already use

Three options compared on fit, benefit, effort and cost, with benefits to be measured in a pilot rather than claimed.

1 · Keep the current approach

No setup effort, but ownership, deadlines and approval checks stay unclear.

Recommended

2 · Standardise the existing Trello workflow

Agree card fields, a named owner, next action and due date, and stage checklists for requirements and approvals. Builds on a tool the team already knows.

3 · Introduce a separate system

Could add reporting and automation, but needs selection, migration, training and subscriptions. The evidence did not justify it for one campaign.

What went well

  • Process maps, use cases and stories made roles and approval points easy to discuss
  • Building on Trello avoided assuming a new system was needed

What was hard

  • No baseline existed for delays, rework or workload
  • Live adoption depends on the station's own decision and owners

What I'd do next time

  • Agree a business owner and required fields before piloting
  • Set baseline measures, then sample live cards weekly
  • Run the 14 scenarios once the setup is agreed
